About Us

Our Story

Coffee Bar – Eatery 28 was born from a passion for great coffee and warm hospitality in Nuwara Eliya’s cool highlands. What started as a vision for an intimate mountain haven has grown into a welcoming retreat where every cup tells a story.

Along the tranquil stretch of Waterfield Drive, we combine the authentic character of Italy’s renowned Lavazza coffee beans with hearty Western breakfast classics and satisfying lunches. Whether you are pausing after a walk around Gregory Lake or starting your highland journey, our doors are open with genuine warmth.
